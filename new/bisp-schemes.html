About Benazir Income Support Programme

Established July 2008 | Federal Social Safety Net

The Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP) is Pakistan’s flagship poverty alleviation initiative. Operating under an advanced digital wallet infrastructure, registered female beneficiaries receive unconditional quarterly financial stipends alongside targeted educational and health grants.

Target Recipient: Deserving Female Family Head
Verification: NADRA Biometric & NSER Survey
Disbursement: Digital Wallet SIMs & Bank Partner Camps
Official SMS: 8171 Shortcode Only

Major BISP Sub-Programmes 2026

Core Unconditional Cash

Benazir Kafaalat Scheme

Quarterly stipend of Rs. 14,500 provided directly to eligible women belonging to households with PMT score under 32.

Digital Wallet SIM Linked
Education Stipend

Benazir Taleemi Wazaif

Conditional cash transfers for children enrolled in Primary to Higher Secondary schools (70% attendance mandatory).

Rs. 3,000 Girl Primary Bonus
Health & Nutrition

Benazir Nashonuma Scheme

Health conditionality stipend for pregnant/lactating mothers and infants up to 2 years to prevent childhood stunting.

Specialized Health Food

Benazir Undergraduate Scholarship

Fully funded university scholarships covering 100% tuition fees plus living stipends for deserving students.

Step-by-Step NSER Registration Guide

1

Visit BISP Tehsil Office

Go to your local BISP Tehsil Registration Center or active NSER Desk with the female household head.

2

Submit Original Documents

Present your original NADRA CNIC and children's official B-Forms to the verification counter staff.

3

Complete Dynamic Survey

Answer socio-economic questions regarding household assets, family size, and utility expenses to generate PMT score.

4

Biometric Verification & SIM Link

Undergo thumbprint biometric validation and register your dedicated Benazir SIM card for digital wallet notifications.

Security Notice

BISP never sends payment messages from private mobile numbers. Only SMS from '8171' is authentic.

  • Never pay registration fees to anyone.
  • Do not share your OTP or bank account PIN.
  • Report fraud to BISP Helpline: 0800-26477.
