Digital Protection Wallets

In 2026, BISP shifted to biometric digital wallet accounts with free Benazir SIM cards, giving female heads direct, safe control over quarterly cash disbursements.

PMT Poverty Score

Eligibility is calculated through the Proxy Means Test (PMT) cut-off score via NSER Dynamic Surveys. Families earning below the PMT threshold qualify for cash stipends.

Taleemi Wazaif & Nashonuma

Extra quarterly education stipends (Primary to Higher Secondary) conditional on 70% school attendance, plus specialized nutrition allowances for mothers and infants.

How to Apply for BISP (NSER Dynamic Registry Workflow)

Step 01

Visit Tehsil Office

Go to your local BISP Tehsil Center or active NSER desk.

Step 02

Document Submission

Present original CNIC of female head & children's NADRA B-Form.

Step 03

Dynamic Survey

Complete socio-economic questionnaire to generate household PMT score.

Step 04

Biometric & SIM

Complete thumbprint verification & obtain free Benazir SIM card.

Anti-Fraud Advisory

BISP and PPSC never request fee payments or bank account details via personal WhatsApp numbers, private SMS, or unauthorized non-`.gov.pk` websites.

  • BISP official SMS string is strictly 8171.
  • PPSC payments are strictly via PSID/1Link.
