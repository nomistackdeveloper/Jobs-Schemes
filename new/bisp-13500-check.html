Overview of BISP 13,500 Installment

The Government of Pakistan has officially disbursed the updated Rs. 13,500 quarterly financial assistance under the Benazir Kafaalat Program for 2026. Eligible beneficiaries can verify their account status, biometric registration status, and payment withdrawal locations directly through the official 8171 portal.

How to Check Eligibility via 8171 SMS

  1. Open the messaging app on your mobile phone.
  2. Type your 13-Digit Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) number without hyphens.
  3. Send the message to official code 8171.
  4. You will receive an automated official response detailing your eligibility status.
