CM Punjab Kisan Card Scheme 2026

Interest-free production loans up to Rs. 150,000 per crop season (calculated at Rs. 30,000 per acre up to 5 acres). Designed for purchasing seeds, DAP fertilizer, and certified pesticides.

Sponsoring Agency: Dept of Agriculture Punjab
Banking Partner: Bank of Punjab (BOP)
Landholding Limit: 1 to 12.5 Acres
Tenor: 6 Months + 1 Month Grace
Full Details & Guidelines Apply Details

CM Punjab Chief Minister Green Tractor Scheme 2026

Government of Punjab provides a direct cash subsidy of Rs. 1,000,000 (10 Lakh) per tractor to eligible farmers possessing 6 to 50 acres of agricultural land.

Subsidy Amount: Rs. 10 Lakh per Unit
Land Requirement: 6 to 50 Acres
Selection Method: Transparent District Balloting
Target Distribution: 9,500+ Tractors

Solar Tubewell Conversion Scheme for Farmers

Government-backed 67% to 80% subsidy program to convert traditional diesel and electric agricultural tubewells into solar-powered pumping systems.

Government Subsidy: Up to 80% Cost Coverage
Target Beneficiaries: Off-Grid & Grid Farmers
Capacity Limit: Up to 15-20 HP Systems
Primary Goal: Reduce Irrigation Fuel Expenses
